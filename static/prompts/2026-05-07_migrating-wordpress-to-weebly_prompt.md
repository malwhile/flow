Write a blog post about transitioning a PTA website from WordPress to Weebly. Include the pros and cons of this migration.

**Pros for transitioning:**
- Weebly is free for our purposes with no ads
- Simpler to maintain
- Easier to have a cohesive look
- Other PTAs in our county are also using it to keep consistent with their look
- No coding required at all
- No special formatting
- True WYSIWYG

**Cons:**
- Less flexible (WordPress has way more options)
- No footer/sidebars/headers that are replicated across all pages
- Less robust plugin marketplace for enhancements

**Tone:** First-person, casual but accurate, light humor focused on the subject matter. Keep it concise (~500-800 words).

**Audience:** PTA members and other non-technical website administrators considering the same migration.
